## Prompts (`prompts.json`)

`prompts.json` is generated by `scripts/make_prompts.py` from upstream sources pinned to fixed commits. Each record has `source`, `source_id` and `license` fields. The prompts are unmodified, except that HumanEval prompts are wrapped in a one-line instruction ("Complete the following Python function...").

Spec-Bench: [Apache-2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0) |
